Map of the United States showing 2026 measles cases per 100,000 residents through October 1, colored by comparison with the national rate of 1.13 per 100,000. Red states are above the national rate and blue states are below it. Utah (15.1) and South Carolina (12.0) are more than eight times the national rate. Pennsylvania (7.4) and North Dakota (4.8) are four to eight times it, and Wisconsin (3.5) is two to four times. Delaware, Kentucky, Virginia, Arizona, Oregon, Ohio and South Dakota are one to two times the national rate. The other 38 states and DC are below it: Texas and Florida are at about half the national rate, 21 jurisdictions including California, Illinois and Georgia are under a quarter of it, and Arkansas, Hawaii, Mississippi, Nevada and New Hampshire reported no cases.
